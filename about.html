ABOUT ME

Technology,
creativity &
curiosity.

I'm Brianna Boone, a Michigan State University graduate with a background in Information Science and Information Technology.

I'm passionate about creating technology that is functional, thoughtful, and engaging. My interests span software development, UX/UI design, cloud technology, networking, and the ways digital systems can solve real-world problems.

01 / MY JOURNEY

Curiosity turned into a career.

My journey in technology began with curiosity—wanting to understand how the digital tools we use every day actually work. As a first-generation college student, I learned to navigate unfamiliar challenges and push myself outside of my comfort zone. I began my education at Wayne County Community College, where I built a strong academic foundation before transferring to Michigan State University and earning my degree in Information Science with a minor in Information Technology.

One of the most meaningful parts of my journey was studying abroad in Tanzania, where I was able to take what I had learned beyond the classroom. I worked on hands-on technology projects involving networking, hardware troubleshooting, Wi-Fi infrastructure, and computer repair while also helping teach programming and digital skills to students and educators.

Working in a new environment challenged me to adapt, communicate, and find practical solutions when resources and circumstances were different from what I was accustomed to.

02 / WHAT'S NEXT

Always learning. Always building.

Since graduating, I've continued expanding my skills through projects in software development, mobile applications, cloud technology, and UX/UI design.

I'm especially interested in opportunities that allow me to bridge software with real-world systems and create technology that is both useful and thoughtfully designed. I see my career as an opportunity to keep learning, take on increasingly complex problems, and build technology that has a meaningful impact.
