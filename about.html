About Me

My Journey

My journey in technology began with curiosity—wondering how the digital tools we use every day actually work. As a first-generation college student, I’ve learned to navigate challenges and push myself outside of my comfort zone. Starting at Wayne County Community College, I built a strong foundation, graduating with honors before transferring to Michigan State University to pursue a degree in Information Science with a minor in Information Technology.

During my study abroad program, I had the opportunity to immerse myself in new cultures and environments while continuing to work on tech projects. From learning Swahili and participating in weekend tours to collaborating on coding and design assignments, the experience broadened my perspective and strengthened my adaptability. It reinforced the importance of teamwork, problem-solving, and approaching challenges creatively—skills I carry into every project I undertake in technology and design.
